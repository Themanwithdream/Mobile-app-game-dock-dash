    Dock Boss

    One parcel. A hundred worlds.

    Three steps to a great delivery

    1. Wait for green

      Watch the lowest, ringed parcel. Tap once it enters the green loading zone. Early taps are safe.

    2. Match the sticker

      Match both its colour and shape to a truck. A golden parcel fits any open truck.

    3. Make it perfect

      The bright centre stripe gives a +5 bonus. Fill a truck with five parcels to dispatch it for double points.
