# Individual world scenery — 6.1

All 100 story worlds use exclusive backdrops in missions and arcade. The original 23 remain individually illustrated; 16 existing biome paintings are now assigned to one matching place each, and 61 new paintings were independently generated using the built-in image generation tool. Stories, saves and sorting rules are unchanged.

New images are resized with nearest-neighbour sampling to 360 × 640 and saved as indexed PNGs. Originals remain unmodified.
