# Historical Scene Artwork

Generated using the built-in image generation tool, then exported as 360×640,
256-colour PNGs using nearest-neighbour sampling for the game's existing pixel
raster. The four scenes share a clear central road for the conveyor and clear
bottom paving for dock controls. New artwork does not replace existing scenes.

## Common Prompt

Use case: historical-scene. One complete 9:16 portrait game background for Dock
Dash, a polished 16-bit pixel-art parcel sorting game. Orthographic top-down
bird's-eye view, crisp square pixel clusters, rich pixel textures, restrained
warm pools of light and dark teal shadows. All interesting scene details belong
in the left and right thirds. Preserve an absolutely clear, straight vertical
stone road through the middle third for the conveyor overlay. Keep the bottom
fifth relatively clear paving for four trucks and controls, and the top fifth
dark and low-detail for the score HUD. No text, writing, UI, conveyor, modern
trucks, central-road characters, perspective tilt, photorealism or smooth 3D.
